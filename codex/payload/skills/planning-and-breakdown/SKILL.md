# Planning and breakdown

Clarify objective, constraints, current state, affected systems, and definition of done. Make assumptions explicit. Decompose work by dependency, with each task naming inputs, owner, expected artifact, validation, risk, and rollback or recovery condition. Separate discovery, design, implementation, test, approval, and deployment rather than treating them as one action.

Mark tasks blocked by missing decisions or authority; do not fill them with invented values. Keep the plan proportionate: a small, reversible code change should not receive a production runbook. Finish with ordered tasks, decision gates, success criteria, and unresolved questions.
